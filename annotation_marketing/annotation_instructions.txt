You are annotating social media marketing posts.

Your task is to assign a rhetorical marketing role to each segment.

Allowed primary labels:
- hook
- pain_point
- solution
- benefit
- proof
- social_proof
- urgency
- scarcity
- objection_handling
- cta
- educational
- storytelling
- uncertain

Definitions:
hook = grabs attention.
pain_point = expresses a user problem, frustration or need.
solution = presents a product, service, method or idea.
benefit = explains the concrete gain for the user.
proof = gives factual evidence, metrics, studies, benchmarks or results.
social_proof = mentions users, clients, testimonials, reviews or adoption.
urgency = pushes the reader to act quickly.
scarcity = mentions limited availability, limited quantity or limited time.
objection_handling = answers a possible hesitation or concern.
cta = explicitly asks the reader to take an action.
educational = teaches, explains or gives useful information.
storytelling = narrates an experience, situation or story.
uncertain = use only if the segment cannot be classified.

Rules:
- Choose exactly one primary_role per segment.
- You may choose zero to two secondary_roles.
- If the segment asks the reader to do something, prefer cta.
- If the segment is a question about a problem, prefer pain_point over hook.
- If the segment contains metrics, numbers, results or studies, prefer proof.
- If the segment mentions clients, users, reviews or testimonials, prefer social_proof.
- If the segment presents the product, service or method, prefer solution.
- If the segment explains what the user gains, prefer benefit.
- If unsure, use uncertain.
- The segments below were produced by a mechanical sentence splitter. If a
  segment still mixes two distinct marketing functions, split it in the
  response, add a new segment_id such as 2.1, and lower the confidence of both
  parts accordingly.
- Return only valid JSON.

Input post (platform: {platform}, language: {language}):
{full_post}

Segments:
{segments}

  "annotations": [
    {{
      "segment_id": 1,
      "text": "...",
      "primary_role": "...",
      "secondary_roles": ["..."],
      "confidence": 0.0,
      "reason": "short explanation"
    }}
  ]
